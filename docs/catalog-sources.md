# Catalog sourcing

Reviewed on **October 17, 2026**. The catalog contains 30 distinct fragrance variants selected from these FragranceNet collection pages:

- https://www.fragrancenet.com/boutiques/best-selling-perfumes
- https://www.fragrancenet.com/boutiques/best-selling-perfumes?page=2

These pages are merchandising lists, change over time, and do not provide verified sales volumes. The catalog is **bestseller-inspired**, not an official top-30 sales ranking. Pagination may change and variants of a fragrance may appear separately. Each catalog record preserves its collection source URL. Brand, fragrance, and concentration names identify the selected variants; descriptive text, categories, packaging art, sample prices, and delivery policy are original demonstration content.

Scent families are broad editorial browsing groups, not manufacturer-verified note pyramids. Prices are fictional USD prices for the demonstration, not FragranceNet prices or offers. No retailer reviews, sales counts, stock figures, or product photos have been copied.

All vial illustrations are original HTML/CSS renderings of generic decants. They do not depict original branded bottles, imply authorized supply, or claim manufacturer-packaged samples. No affiliation with FragranceNet or fragrance brands is asserted.
